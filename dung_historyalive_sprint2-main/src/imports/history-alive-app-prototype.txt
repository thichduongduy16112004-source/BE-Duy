Create a fully interactive mobile app prototype for an educational app called "History Alive".

The prototype must behave like a real mobile app with clickable navigation, transitions, and connected screens.

Use iPhone 15 frame size.

STYLE
- Modern gamified learning design similar to Duolingo
- Bright educational colors
- Primary color: #fbce03
- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Friendly mascot illustrations
- Smooth smart animate transitions
- Bottom navigation bar
